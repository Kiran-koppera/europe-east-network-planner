      Method and limits

      Data

      Timetable: public FlixBus GTFS feed for Europe (gtfs_generic_eu.zip), published by Flixbus France on data.gouv.fr under the ODbL. Population: GeoNames cities15000 (CC BY 4.0). Stops are grouped into cities when they lie within 12 km of a GeoNames city.

      Model

      Weekly departures come from the calendar of a reference week. Demand potential uses a gravity model (population × population / distance², 150–1,200 km), scaled so the strongest candidate scores 100. Its rank correlation with today's frequencies is shown in the header as a fit check.

      Limits

      No ticket or ridership data is public, so the index ranks potential and does not forecast passengers. Occupied and front-line regions of Ukraine are excluded. The dossier is a planning draft; the competent authority's checklist prevails. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Flix.
